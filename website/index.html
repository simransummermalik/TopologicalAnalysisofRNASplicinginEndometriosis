Same gene.
Different connections?

Cells can join pieces of a gene in different ways when making RNA. We’re building Splice Girl to study those connections in endometriosis and control tissue.

The tool turns counted RNA splice junctions into a graph, uses Hodge theory to measure its signal, and saves the results as tables and figures you can inspect.

Start with the biology and graph idea, or try the small demo. You don’t need to know Hodge theory before you start.

What’s working?

The command-line tool and synthetic demo work. We also checked one real gene, CDH11, using one million cleaned read pairs from one control sample. The full eight-sample disease comparison is still in progress.

The demo uses made-up data with known answers. Its case/control labels are examples, not findings about patients.
