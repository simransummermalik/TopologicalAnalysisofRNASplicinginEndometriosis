RNA SPLICING + GRAPH THEORY + ENDOMETRIOSIS

Splice Girl is a student-built tool for studying how pieces of RNA connect. We represent exons or splice sites as nodes, observed splice junctions as edges, and read support as weights.

WHY HODGE THEORY?

Hodge theory helps us separate ordinary start-to-finish flow from signal that remains around alternative paths in the graph. We can then compare those patterns between control and endometriosis samples for the same gene.
